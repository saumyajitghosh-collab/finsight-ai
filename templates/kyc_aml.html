How It Works

The KYC/AML model combines a Random Forest classifier with a rule-based risk scoring engine. It evaluates 13 features including entity type, jurisdiction risk, sanction/PEP links, adverse media, document verification status, and ownership transparency. The system assigns a risk score (0-100) and category with a recommended compliance action.
