How It Works

The Credit Risk model uses XGBoost trained on 10,000 loan applications with features including loan-to-income ratio, credit bureau score, employment type, and tenure. The model outputs a default probability mapped to a risk grade (A–E) with an actionable lending recommendation. SMOTE oversampling addresses class imbalance (13.5% default rate).
